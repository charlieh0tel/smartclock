# 58501A Fiji_EGE: a first look

The 58501A is an early prototype relative of the 58503A,
Ericsson/GE-badged, of 1994 by the date code on its XC3042, 9415
(seen), and the dates its EEPROM's reset records carry, contributed by
its owner, Steve, VE7FM
<steve@ve7fm.com>.  He dumped its firmware and EEPROM, photographed it,
and posted them to the EEVblog forum in 2016 ("Prototype HP/Agilent
GPS based refererence clocks generator",
<https://www.eevblog.com/forum/testgear/prototype-hpagilent-gps-based-refererence-clocks-generator/>),
and has allowed them here (`third_party/NOTICE`).  The firmware is
Hewlett-Packard's.

[`58501a-prototype.html`](https://charlieh0tel.github.io/smartclock/58501a-prototype.html)
is this document as a page, with the photos shown.

- The image: `third_party/firmware/images/58501a-prototype/58501a-fiji_ege.bin`
- The EEPROM, as dumped:
  `third_party/firmware/images/58501a-prototype/58501a-eeprom.bin`
- The photos: `docs/images/58501a-prototype/`

It is not one of the production units the rest of `docs/firmware/`
covers, and stands apart from them: the image is not decompiled, has
no folder in `decompiled/`, `rewritten/` or `analysis/`, appears in
none of the tables that set the images side by side, and none of the
tools that read the production images is extended to it.  Its files
are in folders of their own, so that the checks that take every
`images/*.bin` and every `images/eeprom/*.bin` do not take them.
Comparisons with the 58503A are in this document only.  Nothing here
was run on a 58501A by us, and the 58503A 3704 image has not been
tried on this board.

What is read from the image's code is marked **read**, what is seen in
the photos **seen**, and what follows from either **inferred**.
Addresses are the 58501A's unless another image is named.

## The image

**Read**: 512 KiB from address 0, two 256 KiB banks, the reset vector
at 0 giving the stack `0x10fffe` and the entry `0x28ea`.  The revision
string `Fiji_EGE` is at `0x1e80`, just before the notice "Copyright
Hewlett-Packard Co. 1993. All Rights Reserved." (`0x1e89`); the model
`58501A` is at `0x178b6`.  The kernel is pSOS-68K, "V4.1 COPYRIGHT
1983, 1984, 1985, 1986 SOFTWARE COMPONENTS GROUP" (`0x2bd2`).
**Inferred**: there is no installer.  The reset vector enters the
primary's own start-up, and the ROM's checksums cover the first bank
from its first byte, where the 58503A's leave out the installer's
`0x0` to `0xffff` (below).

Joined from Steve's four chips as `smartclock-cli join-chips` joins the
58503A's (`restart.md`, "The installer", "Flash"): U2 (MSB1) and U17
(LSB1) are bank 1, `0x0` to `0x3ffff`, U1 (MSB2) and U16 (LSB2) bank 2,
the MSB part on the even bytes.  The same 512 KiB were also posted as
two 256 KiB bank files by another forum member.

### The ROM's checksums

**Read**, `0x5916`, called by `0x69de` for bank 1 and `0x6a3a` for bank
2: the bank's even bytes and its odd bytes are summed separately, each
as a 16-bit sum, from the bank's first byte through its `0x3fffb`th, and
compared with the bank's last four bytes, which hold them interleaved:
the even sum's high byte, the odd sum's high byte, the even sum's low
byte, the odd sum's low byte.  A mismatch sets a bit of `0x1007ca`: bits
0 and 1 for bank 1's odd and even lanes, bits 2 and 3 for bank 2's.  The
58503A's rule is the same but that its first bank's sums start at
`0x10000`, past its installer (`restart.md`, "The flasher").  Bank 1's
sums are `0xcbb6` and `0x1f63`, bank 2's `0xe775` and `0x468d`, and both
banks' hold.

### Chip selects

**Read**: the reset code at `0x28ea` sets SYPCR, then writes the chip
selects from `0x28f2` to `0x29aa`; the values it writes are the 58503A
3633 boot code's, in the same order, the 3704's but for CSPAR0, `0x23ff`
here and `0x2bff` there.  The two differ in CSPAR0's CS4 field, bits 11
and 10: `00` here, discrete output, where the 3704's `10` makes the pin
CS4 on an 8-bit port (MC68331UM Table D-10).  The rest is the map
`chip-selects.md` gives the 58503A: the flash in two banks, the RAM at
`0x100000`, the DUART on CS8 at `0x200000`, the FPGA's registers at
`0x304000` on CS5 and the EEPROM on CS9 at `0x400000`.

**Read**, from the code of a few of the 58501A's own words, at the
addresses `dump-pforth` gives them:

- `adc_temp` (`0x8fee`), `adc_5v`, `adc_p15v`, `adc_m15v`, `adc_oven`
  and `adc_doven` (`0x9098`) each call a routine of their own and print

- `prog_freq_out` (`0x91ec`) takes 100, 300 or 600 and stores it at

- `run_diag` (`0x9178`) and `johann` (`0x923c`) each store their number

(`oncore/README.md`, "The message table"), and `smartclock-cli

## The FPGA

**Read**: the image loads one XC3042 bitstream, at `0xcf8a`, 3860 bytes,
SHA-256 `afd3ded7`, the length every image's has (`outputs.md`, "The
FPGA bitstreams").  A table just before the second block points to it
(`0xdea0`).  The second block, at `0xdea8`, is the readback mask,
SHA-256 `d64b97e1`, the same as every other image's, and it follows the
bitstream at the same distance as in the others; `0xf8da` reads the
bitstream through the table and the mask from `0xdead`, five bytes in,
past its header, as the Z3805A's `fpga_verify_chip` does.  The registers
are written through `0x4060a`, the 3704's `fpga_write` (`0x40784`)
instruction for instruction: a byte to `0x304000` plus the register.

The design is its own, not a revision of one held here.  It differs from
the 58503A and Z3801A's design
(`third_party/firmware/images/fpga/58503a-z3801a.bit`) in 3309 of its
3860 bytes, and from the nearest, the 58503B's, in 3248.  Nothing inside
it has been traced.

Its pins are another matter.  Decoded with `tools/fpga/iobdump.js`, of
the 74 bonded pins of its PC84 package, 68 are configured as the
58503A's design configures them.  The six that differ:

<!-- figures_58501a.py pins -->
| Pin | 58501A | 58503A 3704 |
| --- | ------ | ----------- |
| P14 | OUTQ | OUT |
| P17 | PULLUP | I |
| P67 | OUTQ TRI I PULLUP | OUTQ TRI PULLUP |
| P78 | OUT:FAST | OUTQ:FAST |
| P83 | OUTQ | OUTQ:FAST I |
| P84 | PULLUP | OUT:NOT |
<!-- end figures_58501a.py pins -->

OUT is an output, OUTQ a registered one, NOT inverted and FAST fast
slew; TRI a 3-state control; I an input; PULLUP the pad's pull-up, and
a pad with PULLUP alone is unused.  In the 58503A's design
([`58503a-z3801a-trace.md`](58503a-z3801a-trace.md), "Other outputs"),
P17 is an input that P14 passes through unregistered and P84 drives
inverted; P83 and P78 are the internal 1 PPS's two registered outputs,
P83 read back through its own input; and P67 is DATA bit 2, with no
input path.  **Read** from the table: the 58501A's design drives no pin
the 3704's leaves undriven, and leaves one undriven that the 3704's
drives, P84.  Its P14 is registered rather than a passthrough of P17,
which it does not use; its P78 is unregistered; its P83 has neither
fast slew nor its input; and its P67 has an input path.

**Inferred**, for whether the 58503A 3704 image would run on this
board: untested.  Its design would drive P84, which the 58501A's
leaves alone, so if anything on the 58501A's board drives that net,
the two would contend; a continuity check from P84 would settle it.
P17, an input in the 3704's design, may float on this board.  Whether
the 3704's design would make this unit's 19.2 MHz, 9.6 kHz and
programmable outputs is not known; they may come from the hand-built
counter board rather than the FPGA.

## The EEPROM

An AT28C64-sized part, 8 KiB, on chip select 9 at `0x400000`; the
offsets here are from its start.  Steve read it from the socket he
fitted.  **Read** from the image's code, and the dump bears it out
(the figures checked below):

| Offset | What | Checksum | Shown by |
| ------ | ---- | -------- | -------- |
| `0x000` to `0x03f` | erased, `0xff`; no code refers to it | none | |
| `0x040` | a 64-byte record: a date at `+2` and a count at `+6` | 8-bit sum and complement at `+0` | `0x15eba`, read by `0x15ee6`, written by `0x15f26` |
| `0x080`, `0x0c0` | the configuration block's two slots, 64 bytes each | 16-bit sum at `+2` | `0x17324`, checked by `0x1754a`, slots chosen by `0x17284` |
| `0x100` to `0xc7f` | the reset records, 32 of `0x5c` bytes | 8-bit sum and complement at `+2` | `0x178c0`, read by `0x178ee` |
| `0xc80` to the end | the log, pages of `0x40` bytes | 8-bit sum and complement at `+2` | `0x164f8`, written by `0x16522` |

Nothing in the EEPROM names the unit: there is no identification
record, model or serial number, as the 58503A's has at `0x010`
(`structures.md`, "EEPROM records").  Its `*IDN?` serial field is `0`.

### The checksums

The **8-bit sum and complement** is the other images'
`record_checksum` rule (`structures.md`, "EEPROM records"): add the
covered bytes modulo 256 into *s*; the stored word is *s* in its high
byte and the complement of *s*, `0xff - s`, in its low byte, so the two
bytes always add to `0xff`.

- The record at `0x040`: the word at `+0` over `+2` to `+0x3f`
  (`0x15eba`).  Its date and count are those of the 58503A's lifetime
  record (`structures.md`, "The lifetime record"): with a bad checksum
  the reader (`0x15ee6`) sets 1994-01-01, `0x07ca0101`, and 0.
- A reset record: the word at `+2` over `+4` to `+0x5b` (`0x178c0`),
  the `reset_record` layout and `reset_record_checksum` rule of the
  other images
  (`third_party/firmware/rewritten/common/types/reset_record.h`).  The
  first three records hold.  The next three carry text, but their
  sequence and checksum words are zero, and fail; the rest are erased.
- A log page: the word at `+2` over `+4` to `+0x3f` (`0x164f8`); the
  word at `+0` is one more than the one the page held before
  (`0x16560`).  Every page in the dump holds.

The **configuration block's 16-bit sum**, **read** from `0x17324` and
its callers: add the bytes at `+8` through `+0x3f`, each zero-extended,
into a 32-bit sum; the checksum is its low 16 bits, stored big-endian
at `+2`.  The tag at `+0` and the sequence at `+4` are not covered.
The writer, `0x1734a`, stores it with `move.w` (`0x174d6`); the
checker, `0x1754a`, compares it with `cmp.w` (`0x1756c`).

A slot is valid (`0x1754a`) when all three hold: the word at `+0` is
`0x5313`; the sum above equals the word at `+2`; and the eight bytes at
`+8` equal the image's revision string, `Fiji_EGE` at `0x1e80`
(`strncmp`, `0x4b738`, eight bytes).  A block written by other firmware
is therefore never taken, even with its checksum right.

### The configuration block

| Offset | What |
| ------ | ---- |
| `+0x00` | `0x5313`, the tag; invalidating a slot writes `0xff` over its first byte (`0x1724c`) |
| `+0x02` | the 16-bit sum |
| `+0x04` | a 32-bit sequence: the writer stores the current slot's plus one (`0x1736a`) |
| `+0x08` | the revision, eight bytes, `Fiji_EGE` |
| `+0x10` to `+0x3f` | the settings, packed |

The settings are packed by two tables of field descriptors, eight at
`0x17718` and six at `0x17738`, most significant bit first into 32-bit
words, a field never crossing a word.  Each descriptor gives a width,
signed or not, a count, and the RAM it is loaded into (`0x1759a`
unpacks, `0x1734a` packs).  The one identified is the position: three
signed 32-bit fields at `+0x1c`, `+0x20` and `+0x24`, latitude and
longitude in milliarcseconds and height in centimeters, by their
descriptor's bounds (`0x17ec6`): ±90° and ±180° in milliarcseconds,
-1,000 m to 18,000 m in centimeters.  The rest is not identified.

`0x17284`, called once (`0x1bfe`), picks the slot: slot 0 if valid;
else slot 1 if valid, and then slot 0 is invalidated; else neither is,
and it sets `0x1007ba`, loads defaults (`0x15e88`, `0x31` bytes, to
`0x1012c8`), in which the position is zero, and has `0x1734a` write
them to one slot, invalidating the other.

In the dump, slot 0 is valid, and its position decodes to 37.35865472°,
-79.17463639°, 270.23 m, as Steve read it.  Slot 1 is not: its first
byte is `0xff`, and its revision is `Fiji` with four zero bytes after
it, though its sum holds.

**Why the thread's two-byte edit made the unit ignore the EEPROM**:
Steve changed the height, `0x698f` at `0x0a4` (slot 0's `+0x24`), and
nothing else.  The height is inside the sum's span, `+8` to `+0x3f`,
so slot 0's word at `+2` no longer matched and `0x1754a` refused the
slot; slot 1 was already invalid; so `0x17284` took the defaults, with
a zero position.  To change a field, recompute the sum over `+8` to
`+0x3f` and store its low 16 bits at `+2`.  That is **read**; it was
not tried on the unit.

## The checks

`make check-58501a-figures` (`tools/firmware/figures_58501a.py`)
recomputes the figures above that come from a tool's reading, from the
image, the EEPROM and the 58503A images and bitstream: the checksums,
the position, the chip selects, the pForth words, with their counts and
the word table from `smartclock-cli dump-pforth` run on this image and
on each production image, the Oncore messages, the bitstream's place,
hash and differences, and the six pins from `tools/fpga/iobdump.js`,
run with the XC3000 viewer cloned at the pinned commit, and fails if a
sentence that quotes one is not in this document as it computes it,
once, or if either table differs from what it generates.  It checks the
SHA-256s in
`third_party/NOTICE` too.  `make pages` runs it before it renders
this document's page, so the Pages build fails on a stale figure.
