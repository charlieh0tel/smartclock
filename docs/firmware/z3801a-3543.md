# Z3801A 3543 against Z3805A 3543B

The Z3801A's image, `third_party/firmware/z3801a-3543.bin` (revision
3543), ported from the Z3805A 3543B as the 58503A 3704 was
([`58503a-3704.md`](58503a-3704.md)): an export from a bare import,
names seeded from the Z3805A's by `image_diff.py`'s matches, then the
C.  Its folder is `third_party/firmware/z3801a-3543/`.  It shares
`common/` with the Z3805A and the 58503A ([`rewrite.md`](rewrite.md),
"Shared C").

## The base

`image_diff.py` against the Z3805A
([`diff/z3801a-3543-vs-z3805a-3543b.tsv`](diff/z3801a-3543-vs-z3805a-3543b.tsv)),
the Z3801A's functions by how they match, and their bytes:

| Region | Identical | Relocated | Changed ≥ 0.70 | Changed < 0.70 | New | Missing |
| ------ | ----: | ----: | ----: | ----: | ----: | ----: |
| primary functions | 628 | 1018 | 21 | 6 | 4 (88 B) | 27 |
| installer functions | 341 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

98.2 % of the primary's functions are carried as they stand, the
installer whole.  The Z3801A is seeded from the Z3805A
(`seed_names.py`'s `PORT_Z3801A_3543`; `make seed-names`), and its
types start as the Z3805A's `smartclock.h` and `check-layout.c`.

## Layout

The Z3805A's.  The two images' first 64 KiB, the boot code and the
installer's records, are the same bytes: the installer is unpacked
from `0xbbc` and entered at `0x10135c`, the primary's vectors are at
`0x10000`, the chip selects and port set-up are the Z3805A's
(`chip-selects.md`).  The primary is shorter: its
code moves down by up to `0x11fc`, its RAM by 2 to `0x184` bytes, the
SCI's state and the ES message's gone.

## How it differs from the Z3805A

- **No SCI.**  The SCI driver (`sci.c`, 20 functions), its
  exchanges, ring and driver state, and `sci_settings` are missing.
  The I/O table has three devices, DUART channel B, the QSPI and the
  console's string device (`io_table`, `0x1025b2`, from `0x516d8`),
  so `psos_config_init` copies `0x6c` bytes where the Z3805A's copies
  `0x90`.  `SERial2`'s nodes share `SERial1`'s handlers, channel B's;
  `save_parameter_record` stores channel B's settings in the record's
  SCI bytes too.
- **No ES message.**  `es_build`, `es_schedule` and their buffer are
  missing; `timecode_update` builds the time code alone.
- **The FPGA's bits.**  `fpga_bit_table` (`0x41322`) has ten entries:
  four flags in register 2 before sync, which only
  `fpga_fields_to_registers` (`0x40afe`) and
  `counter_configuration_init` (`0x40ed8`) touch, the latter setting
  the third.  Sync is entry 9 (`counter_set_sync`, `+0x24`).  What the
  four drive is not established.
- **Status.**  `status_summary_update` and `status_summary` are gone.
  The alarm preset's enables and the transition filters differ in one
  word each (`0x2e674` on), as do `nv_settings_defaults`
  (`0x2f860`): the fourth enable 1 where the Z3805A's is 3, the second
  positive-transition filter 0 where it is 2, and
  `douglas_state_powerup` 0.
- **Identity.**  Revision `3543` (`revision_text`, `0x12eee`), model
  `Z3801A`, the records' tag `Q` (`identification_tag`, `0x31df9`)
  where the Z3805A's is `S`; channel B's defaults 19200 baud, odd
  parity, seven bits, no echo (`duartb_settings_defaults`,
  `0x2f92a`).
- **The event log.**  "First satellite tracked at" prints its time as
  `H%08X` (`0x30b2c`); `log_read` follows.
- **SCPI.**  `:LED:ALARm:USER` has real handlers (`0x2c85a`,
  `0x2c88a`), where the Z3805A's refuse it; the `SERial1`
  `:RECeive` and `:FDUPlex` handlers and `:DIAGnostic:SYSTem:PSTartup?`
  differ; `scpi_group_cells` has no `SERial1` word (10 bytes).

## The seeding's exceptions

`copyright_text` is dropped where `image_diff` places it, inside the
installer's records (`0x14b2`), which hold the same text; the
Z3805A's `installer_records` row gives way to the one read from this
image.  127 hand rows ("written by hand for the Z3801A 3543"): 81 RAM
labels the rules leave unplaced, each where its neighbors moved and
referred to as often as the Z3805A's; ROM labels found by their bytes
(the log texts), by the NV tables' pointers (six NV descriptors) or by
the matched code's references (the GPS scripts, `0x42` lower); and
`copyright_text`, `revision_text`, `identification_tag`,
`io_table_defaults` and `io_table`.

## The C

`common/` as for the other two.  Three of its functions differ in the
Z3801A and moved to each image's own C first (rule 6):
`counter_set_sync`, and `scpi_set_LED_ALARm_USER` and
`scpi_query_LED_ALARm_USER`; and `fpga_set_bit` and `fpga_set_field`,
whose table differs, with each image's whole `fpga_bits`
(`common/fpga.c` keeps the entries every image starts with).
`FPGA_BIT_SYNC` left the shared head of `fpga_bit_index`.

`rewritten/` is the Z3805A's per-image C less the SCI driver
(`sci.c`, `bindings/sci_token.h`), `es_build`, `es_schedule`,
`sci_settings_preset` and `status_summary_update`; with the image's
revision, model, tag, defaults and console port's object
(`SCPI_CONSOLE_PORT`, `0x28cdc`), its ten FPGA entries, and its
comments' addresses moved to the Z3801A's.  Of the changed functions,
`psos_config_init`, `counter_configuration_init`, `counter_set_sync`,
`fpga_fields_to_registers`, `sample_action_print`,
`format_satellite_cell` and `log_status` are the Z3805A's C as it
stands, against the Z3801A's types; the other changed functions, and
the four new, are the decompiler's reading.  `smartclock.h` and `check-layout.c` are
the Z3805A's with the Z3801A's addresses and its own `fpga_bit_index`,
`scpi_group_cells_fields` and three-device `io_table`, without the
SCI's types.  The installer's C is the Z3805A's.
