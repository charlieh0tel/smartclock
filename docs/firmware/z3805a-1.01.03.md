# Z3805A 1.01.03 against 58503B 1.01.04 and Z3815A 4010

The Z3805A's 1.01.03 image, `third_party/firmware/images/z3805a-1.01.03.bin`
(revision 1.01.03), read from a Z3805A's flash with `smartclock-cli
read-flash` (`third_party/NOTICE`).  The image and EEPROM were provided by Walt.
It is a Z3805A whose GPS engine is a Furuno, not the Motorola Oncore
of the Z3805A 3543B
([`z3805a-trace.md`](z3805a-trace.md), `gps.md`): its code speaks
Furuno's `$PFEC` sentences, as the Z3815A 4010's does
([`z3815a-4010.md`](z3815a-4010.md)), and its SCPI tree and most of
its code are the 1.01 line's, the 58503B 1.01.04's
([`58503b-1.01.04.md`](58503b-1.01.04.md)).  Its folder is
`third_party/firmware/analysis/z3805a-1.01.03/`.  The first wave seeded
its names from the 58503B's, and for the Furuno code the 58503B has no
counterpart of from the Z3815A's as a second base; the second names
its installer from the Z3805A 3543B's, whose installer it is.  The
image shares `common/` since the join ("Joining `common/`").

What is read from the image is marked **read**, what follows from it
**inferred**; "Not established" lists what neither settles.  Addresses
are the Z3805A 1.01.03's unless another image is named.

The 58503B and the Z3815A carry the primary all but equally: the
58503B's matches carry more functions, identical and relocated, and
more of the bytes, the Z3815A's more with the changed ones and leave
fewer new.  The base is the 58503B, whose matches carry the most
functions, the rule `PLAN.md` records ("Seeding a port's names").  The
Z3815A is the port's second base (`seed_names.py`'s
`second_base_folder`; the names file's header gives the rule): of the
functions the 58503B's matches find new, its matches carry the most
(`image_diff.py`'s new rows in the 58503B's diff, looked up in each
other seeded image's), its Furuno code among them.  A function the
58503B's matches find new is carried from the Z3815A's where those
carry it and its name is free, each row's note ending "from the Z3815A
4010, by image_diff as the second base"; `make seed-names` lists them
under "Rows from the second base".

The Z3805A 3543B carries the fewest of the primary's functions, but
its installer is this image's byte for byte: the first 64 KiB, the
boot code and the installer's records, are the 3543B's (and the
Z3801A 3543's) bytes (**read**), where the 58503B's and the Z3815A's
first 128 KiB hold the Vatican installer.  It is the port's
installer's base (`seed_names.py`'s `installer_base_folder`; the names
file's header gives the rule): the 58503B names the primary alone, and
the 3543B the installer, its rows' notes ending "from the Z3805A 3543B,
by image_diff as the installer's base", which `make seed-names` lists
under "Rows from the installer's base", the RAM labels the installer's
code places among them.  Its diff is kept for the layout too ("Layout
and chip selects").

The Z3805A 1.01.03 is seeded from the 58503B (`seed_names.py`'s
`PORT_Z3805A_1_01_03`; `make seed-names`), its installer from the
3543B, with two exceptions (the names file's header).  As the 58503B's seeding needed, two bare `rts`
whose bytes match at more than one address are pinned where this

`pulse_program` to `0x43308` and `0x4330a`, which `output_state_done`
and `pulse_settings_done` call (`0x43372`, `0x43360`).  Three routines
image_diff pairs a few instructions past their starts are pinned to
them: `sci_puts` to `0x1f7cc`, its test of a zero length, which
`sci_write` and `sci_putc` call (`0x20270`, `0x202d4`), and
`sci_reconfigure` to `0x200da`, its link, where the I/O table's entry

to `0x2309e`, which `pps_monitors_reset` calls (`0x251bc`), its clear
of `oven_warmup_bypassed`.  And a start
image_diff finds new is data, left out: `0x5588a`, the words before
`psos_config_defaults`.

Its types started as the 58503B's `smartclock.h` and `check-layout.c`
(`rewritten/z3805a-1.01.03/types/`), their layouts, addresses and
comments still the 58503B's but for the two records the export needed
("The export and the checks").

made with them:

  board, the Z3805A's, has one, and it also has the Z3815A's SCI

  base.  Hand rows name the serial routines by the port each drives
  here, where the 58503B's rows name them for its ACIA and its SCI

  channel B: channel A's driver, the GPS engine's, `drta_send_message`,
  `duarta_rx_pending`, `drta_get_byte`, `duarta_isr` and
  `duarta_tx_interrupt` (`0x1e272` to `0x1eb9a`), as the Z3805A
  3543B's and the Z3815A's, which their matches pair relocated, where
  the 58503B's are `acia_*`; channel B's, device 0's, transmit

  named as the Z3815A's lays it, by hand rows: channel A's ring and

## Provenance

**Read** from the image: `smartclock-cli read-flash`'s dump, 512 KB
from address 0 (`third_party/NOTICE`).  The image's own revision
string is `1.01.03` (`0x13048`), before the primary's notice,
"Copyright Hewlett-Packard Co. 1993-1997" (`0x13050`); the boot code's
notice is the 3543B's, 1993 (`0x14b2`).  Whether the Furuno units left
the factory so or were converted later, nothing in the image settles
("Not established").

## Layout and chip selects

**Read.**  The first 64 KiB, the boot code and the installer's
records, are the Z3805A 3543B's bytes, which the Z3801A 3543's are too;
the installer is unpacked and entered as theirs (`0xbbc`, `0x10135c`;
`README.md`, "Decompiling with Ghidra").  The primary's vector table is
at `0x10000`, as the 3543B's, its reset entry `0x14820`, where the
58503B's and the Z3815A's are at `0x20000`, behind the Vatican
installer.

The chip selects ([`chip-selects.md`](chip-selects.md), "Z3805A
1.01.03") are the Z3805A 3543B's map, not the 58503B's or the
Z3815A's: two banks of flash, `0x000000` and `0x040000`, read through
CSBOOT and CS1 and written through CS6 and CS7; the RAM at
`0x100000`; the DUART on CS8 at `0x200000`; CS5's 64 KiB at
`0x300000`; the EEPROM on CS9 at `0x400000`; CS4 at `0x500000`; and
CS10 the level 2 interrupt acknowledge.  The boot code writes the
3543B's values; the primary writes them too but CSBAR4 and CSOR4,
which keep the boot code's, the same values.

The kernel's I/O table (`io_table`, `0x1033c4`, copied from `0x55d48`
by `psos_config_init`, `0x5569a`) has four devices in the 3543B's
order: DUART channel B (`duart_init`, `0x1d9e2`), the QSPI, the
string device, whose write is `drtb_write`'s, and the SCI (`sci_init`,
`0x1fe7a`).  The 58503B, which has no DUART, puts the SCI first.

## The GPS engine

**Read.**  A Furuno, on NMEA, not an Oncore: the image holds no Oncore
message table (`smartclock-cli dump-oncore` refuses it, and it is one
of the root `Makefile`'s `FURUNO_IMAGES`), and its framer, sentence
decoders and encoders are the Z3815A 4010's, carried from it as the
second base (the `nmea_` rows of `names.tsv`; [`z3815a-4010.md`](z3815a-4010.md)
for what each does).  DUART channel A is set up as the Z3805A 3543B's
and the Z3815A's: MR1A `0x13`, MR2A `0x07`, CSRA `0xbb`
(`duart_init`, `0x1da72` to `0x1daa4`), eight bits, no parity, one
stop bit, 9600 baud (MC68681 data sheet, `third_party/motorola/MC68681.pdf`,
the mode registers and the baud rate table).

Against the Z3815A's GT-74 path (`z3815a-4010.md`, "The GPS wave"),
from the two images' sentence and request tables:

- **Sentences decoded.**  The Z3815A's ten, `GGA`, `GSA`, `GSV`,
  `anc`, `ssd`, `tst`, `tps`, `rrm`, `rsd` and `RMC`, and one more,
  `acc` (the names at `0x54e66` to `0x54e8e`, where the Z3815A's are
  at `0x5e398` to `0x5e3bc`).
- **Requests.**  The Z3815A's, `set,M`, `srq`, `ZDA`, `GLL`, `set,H`,
  `set,U`, `set,Z`, `set,t`, `rrs`, `rrq`, `clr`, the `int,` requests
  and `zzz`, and two more, `set,X` and `int,acc` (`0x5515e` to
  `0x551ea`); the periodic interval request is
  `$PFEC,GPint,anc00,acc00` (`0x4f7fa`), where the Z3815A's asks for
  `anc00` alone.  `set,X`'s descriptor (`0x54f3e`), among the
  requests' `0x20`-byte entries, names `nmea_encode_digit`
  (`0x529d4`) for its field, one argument as one digit; which caller
  sends it is not traced.  `acc` is the GT-8031's "SV accuracy" (GT
  p. 28), a hex digit or `X` for each satellite.  The request codes
  are this image's own (`smartclock.h`'s `gps_request_code`): its
  sentences `0x40` to `0x55`, their descriptors `0x5469e` plus `0x20` a
  code (`gps_request_descriptor`, `0x5286a`), the Z3815A's seven codes
  on with `set,X` at `0x45` and `int,acc` at `0x50`; its steps `0x56`
  to `0x75` (`gps_request_class`, `0x4daf2`), the Z3815A's nine codes on
  to the survey's stop and eleven from the PPS check, with two of its
  own between, `0x67`, which the GPS task does nothing for (`0x4e882`;
  `GPS_STEP_67`, a placeholder name), and `0x68`, which clears
  `gps.surveying` while `gps.field_592`'s bit 0 is set (`0x4e7da`),
  and a third at `0x6e`, the Z3815A's empty `0x63`, which sets
  `gps.poll_monitor_enabled` (`0x4e83e`); and its scripts `0x76` to
  `0x8e`, the Z3815A's eleven codes on, whose lookup's jump table this
  image's is (`0x4db5a`).  Below `0x40` the codes are the Oncore's,
  which the GPS task translates by `gps_request_translations`
  (`0x551f4`, eight bytes an entry, to the end at `0x55254`): `@@Ag` to
  `set,M`, `@@Aa` and `@@Ac` to the time's and date's scripts, `@@Am`
  to `set,U`, `@@Ad` and `@@Ae` to the latitude's and longitude's
  scripts, `@@Af` to `set,H`, `@@As` to `GLL`, `@@At` to `set,Z`, `@@Az`
  to `set,t`, `@@Aw` to the UTC step, each the Z3815A's, and `0x2f` to
  the defaults script (`0x5524c`), where the Z3815A's takes `0x2e`.
  `gps_request_translate` tests `0x2f` too (`0x5422a`),
  `pll_scpi_send_to_loop` sends it (`0x4366c`), and
  `gps_message_supported` takes `0x29` and `0x35` for Time RAIM
  (`0x52834`, `0x5283a`), where the Z3815A's takes `0x29` and `0x34`:
  the codes above `@@Bn` are the Z3816A's and the 58503B's, one higher
  than the 58503A's and the Z3815A's, `@@Cf` at `0x2f` and `@@En` at
  `0x35`.  The others above `@@Bn` nothing here sends, names or
  decodes; their names are that numbering's.
- **The engine's model.**  The Z3815A names its engine GT-74 whatever
  answers (`nmea_tst_commit`, `0x5d298`, copies "MODEL # FURUNO
  GT-74").  This image's counterpart, `0x53c12`, takes the model from
  the engine's software part number in its `tst` answer: 4850266 gives
  "MODEL # FURUNO GT-80" and sets bits 15 and 14 of the GPS flags word
  at `0x1019fc` to 2, 4850207 "MODEL # FURUNO GT-77" and 1, any other
  "MODEL # FURUNO GT-74" and 0 (`0x53c6a` to `0x53cc4`), the top two
  bits of `gps.field_592`, the word's high byte.  It then calls
  `gps_channel_count_from_model` (`0x5279a`), which makes those bits the
  channel count, `gps.channel_count`: 6 for the GT-74, 8 for the GT-77,
  12 for the GT-80 (`0x527a0` to `0x527d2`).  `selftest_gps`
  (`0x181d4`) reports the self-test word's channel 7 and 8 correlation
  bits only for a GT-77 or GT-80, and goes from channel 6 to the 1 kHz
  presence for a GT-74.  The command log's title stays "GT-74 Command
  Log" (`0x51219`).
- **Twelve channels.**  The records the commits fill hold twelve
  channels where the Z3815A's hold eight: the status's channels, whose
  receiver status follows at `+0x56` (`nmea_anc_channels_commit`,
  `0x53920`), the extension's URAs (`gps_print_extension`), the time
  RAIM record's channels, `raim_received` `0x64` bytes on (the rrm
  commit, `0x53bda`), and the alarm's channels after it ("Types").
- **anc and acc.**  `anc`'s satellites have a decoder of their own,
  `nmea_decode_anc_satellites` (`0x53468`), which also flags any
  satellite marked `2`; `nmea_anc_commit` (`0x53db2`) takes them, with
  `ssd`'s ignored satellites, into the visibility record as the
  Z3815A's does, and `nmea_anc_channels_commit` (`0x53904`) onto the
  status's channels, bit 4, the flag into the receiver status, bit 0.
  `acc`'s satellites go onto the channels too, bit 3
  (`nmea_acc_commit`, `0x53970`); its field's decoder,
  `nmea_decode_satellite_bits` (`0x534be`), takes a satellite for each
  hex digit above `6`, by its place in the field (`0x534d8` to
  `0x534f8`), where the Z3815A's takes one for each `1`.  Bit 3 is an
  Oncore's "satellite reported inaccurate" (VP p. 73), as bit 4, where
  `anc` marks a satellite unhealthy, is its "satellite reported
  unhealthy".  Nothing in the image tests bit 3: only the console's
  status lines print it (`docs/firmware/gps-fields.tsv`).  The tps
  commit runs both after the GSV commit (`0x53e82`, `0x53e8a`).
- **ssd and rsd.**  `ssd` has a field the Z3815A's does not decode,
  field 4, an `E` and a digit (`nmea_decode_ssd_e`, `0x535ee`), which
  nothing reads.  The `rrm` and `rsd` sentences have a table commit,
  `nmea_pps_mode_commit` (`0x536ae`), where the Z3815A's have none: for
  `rsd` it sets `gps.field_592`'s bit 2 while the PPS mode is 1, which
  `gps_pps_check` tests (`0x24c84`).
- **The cable delay correction** is the tst commit's: -400 for software
  4850102 at version 8, else 0 (`0x53d52` to `0x53d6c`), where the
  Z3815A's is a routine of its own.

No path in the image frames Oncore binary: the Z3815A's
`gps_receive`, which frames a sentence through the NMEA framer's
states, is this image's `0x4d502`, relocated (the diff against the
Z3815A), and no `@@` message ID is in a table (`gps.md`, "Six or eight
channels").  The Oncore code the 1.01 line carries is still in the
image, as in the Z3815A, its readers of the 58503A's records among it
(`oncore_position_message`, the receiver ID's commit at `0x51ba2`,
which sets the GT-77's value in `gps.field_592`); the records the NMEA
commits fill are the 58503A's, so that code reads what they leave.

## What differs

From the three diffs and the image's code.

- **From the Z3805A 3543B**, whose board, boot code, installer, chip
  selects and FPGA design it shares: the GPS engine, a Furuno on NMEA
  where the 3543B's is a six-channel Oncore on binary; the SCPI tree,

## The FPGA

**Read.**  The image carries the pair of 3860-byte XC3000-family
blocks every image does, its design at `0x21044` and the common
readback mask at `0x21f62`: the design is the Z3805A 3543B's,

does.  `cargo test -p smartclock-cli --test fpga_bitstreams` checks
