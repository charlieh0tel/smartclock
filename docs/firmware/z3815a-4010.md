# Z3815A 4010 against 58503A 3704 and Z3805A 3543B

The Z3815A's image, `third_party/firmware/images/z3815a-4010.bin` (revision
4010), ported as the 58503A 3704 was ([`58503a-3704.md`](58503a-3704.md)):
an export from a bare import, names seeded from a named image by
`image_diff.py`'s matches, and the harness's checks.  Its folder is
`third_party/firmware/analysis/z3815a-4010/`.  It is the fourth image to
share `common/` (`rewrite.md`, "Shared C"); the waves that read its own
code follow.

The 58503A is the closer: 4010 is of its generation, with its port
layer, its `PON`, its health monitor's table and the USA installer the
Vatican one copies.  The Z3815A is seeded from it
(`seed_names.py`'s `PORT_Z3815A_4010`; `make seed-names`), and its
types start as the 58503A's `smartclock.h`.  Of the functions new
against the 58503A, the Z3805A matches 14 as they stand (866 bytes):

The diff against the Z3805A is kept beside the seeding's, for the

## How it differs from the Z3805A

From the two diffs and the image's code; the per-module counts are in
the TSVs (`cut -f1-3,7`).

- **Layout.**  The boot code and the Vatican installer fill the first
  128 KiB: the primary's vector table is at `0x20000`, not `0x10000`
  (`SmartClockSetup.java` now finds it), the installer's records at
  `0x8000`, unpacked to `0x100400` and entered at `0x1013a6` (boot
  vector 43, `0x492`; the jump at `0x8ec`).  The boot check sums words
  of one 16-bit part, as the Z3816A's (`restart.md`, "The installer", "Boot check").
- **Chip selects.**  The Z3816A's map (`README.md`'s table;
  `chip-selects.md`): CS1 a 16-bit write latch at `0x300000`, CS7 the
  switch word at `0x302000`, read in ten places, bits 7, 12, 13 and 14,
  bit 12 picking τ and G (`restart.md`, "The switch byte at `0x302000`"), CS5 the
  FPGA at `0x304000`, CS10 at `0x500000`, CS4 the level-2 acknowledge.
- **Host port.**  The primary's I/O table (`0x5f1be`, four devices)
  puts the SCI at device 0, the console's, whose write the string
  device, device 2, shares (`sci_write`, `0x3081a`), then the QSPI, and
  DUART channel B at device 3 (`duart_init`, `0x2e00c`), the SCPI port's
  (`scpi_task` passes `duartb_port_write`, `0x2e542`): the other images'
  channel B is device 0 and their SCI, where they have one, device 3.
  The Z3805A's SCI driver matches it, 11 of its routines relocated and
  4 changed; the 58503A has none.  The Z3805A's device-3 wrappers match
  this image's channel B ones byte for byte (`duartb_port_write` is its
  `sci_port_write`), and the 58503A's device-0 ones, named for channel
  B, are this image's SCI's (`duartb_settings_done`, `0x309ac`,
  reconfigures the SCI here); the names follow the code they were
  seeded from.  The Vatican installer drives the SCI
  too: its DUART B interrupt and output routines are missing against
  both bases, and two routines of its own, `installer::0x1033ec` and
  `installer::0x103a88`, program and poll the SCI's registers.
- **GPS.**  A Furuno GT-74 on NMEA (`gps.md`), not an Oncore:
  `applications/gps` is 115 functions new against the Z3805A, 49
  changed and 18 missing.  Its record, `gps` (`0x100f2c`, `0x678`
  bytes), is the 58503A's records at this image's own offsets, which
  the carried code reads, and the GT-74's sentences after the receiver
  ID, from which the sentences' commits fill them (structures.md, "The
  GPS task's state"; below).
- **The oscillator and references.**  One OCXO ("Internal OCXO Fail",
  ": OCXO warm-up"), a module the code calls the puck, on a bit-serial
  link: a task clocks PE7 once a pass and shifts in PE5 while PE2 is
  high (`0x3658e`), a frame headed `0x55` (six bytes) or `0xa1` (seven)
  and checked by a sum (`0x36502`).  A good frame marks the link alive
  (`0x102387`, read by `0x35f98`: the pForth word `puck`, `0x35fa0`,
  prints "puck communication is alive", and the health check logs
  "OCXO Communication Fail" when it lapses with the switch word's bit
  12 set), and `0x3622c` decodes a `0x55` frame: a 24-bit EFC ADC
  reading (`0x1021d6`) and a status byte, bit 0 "puck warm"
  (`0x36054`).  Per frame `0x3607c` compares that reading with
  `0x102374`, the EFC in the ADC's counts (the EFC times
  `:DIAG:ROSC:EFC:DADC`, `0x36386`), divides the difference by `:DADC`
  (`0x1023c4`) into a correction, and logs "EFC ADC out of range"; the
  OCXO calibration's a and b, `:ASLOPe` and `:BSLOPe` ("OCXO cal, a= %e",
  b, "dac/adc"), are at `0x1023c8` and `0x1023cc`, all three kept in the
  calibration block (the loop wave, below).  Two ovens, by name:
  ": internal oven warmup" and ": external oven warmup" are power-up
  sub-states the console's `pr_pll` names (`diag_pr_pll`, `0x2c198`),
  likely a double-oven OCXO's inner and outer.
  The 1PPS reference is GPS or external ("switched to GPS 1PPS
  reference", "switched to Ext 1PPS reference", "EXT reference valid",
  "Holdover started, invalid EXT 1PPS").  Each 1PPS, on an odd GPS
  second, pulses the FPGA's seconds phase bit, `FPGA_BIT_SECONDS_PHASE`
  (`pps_sync_pulse`, `0x516de`, which `pps_interrupt` calls where the
  58503A's signals its front panel).  The loop's state,
  `pll` (`0x10286e`), is larger and its own, typed whole by the loop
  wave (below), its n3 lock pll stage's fields among them.
  `applications/pll` is 30 new against the Z3805A, 42 changed.
- **QSPI device 0** is six 8-bit transfers (`0x30d48`), where the
  Z3805A's sends three of 12 and the 58503A's one of 8
  (`structures.md`, "The QSPI's devices").
- **Status snapshot.**  Its record grew to `0x176` bytes, the 58503A's
  fields reordered and six of its own, the card's heading and alarms
  among them, and there are four, two for the screen and the two
  `status_snapshot_update` takes (below).
- **Libraries.**  The float library, the pSOS kernel and the pForth
  kernel are the Z3805A's (`unassigned/*`, 283 identical, 84
  relocated); one double routine is new after `vsprintf`
  (`0x709ac`, working with ln 2 / 2).
