# Z3816A 4001 against 58503A 3704 and Z3815A 4010

The Z3816A's image, `third_party/firmware/images/z3816a-4001.bin`
(revision 4001; `third_party/NOTICE`), the image [`README.md`](README.md)'s
addresses are in, laid out as the Z3815A 4010's and the 58503B's are.
It carries a Motorola Oncore's code, as the 58503A does (`gps.md`;
`oncore/z3816a-4001.txt`), and takes the language command on its one
host port (`console.md`, "The 58503A image").  Its folder is
`third_party/firmware/analysis/z3816a-4001/`.  The first wave seeded its
names from the 58503A 3704's, as the Z3817A's were seeded from the
Z3815A's ([`z3817a-3752.md`](z3817a-3752.md)), with the Z3815A 4010 as
the second base, made the export and its checks, typed what the export
showed to be otherwise and ran the function check, the image
unfinished; the waves after it brought the function check to no
failures, and the join made it the sixth image to share `common/`,
every function of it written by hand ("Joining `common/`").

What is read from the image is marked **read**, what follows from it
**inferred**; "Not established" lists what neither settles.  Addresses
are the Z3816A's unless another image is named.

The 58503A carries more of the primary, by count and more so by bytes:
the Z3816A frames Oncore binary as the 58503A does, where the Z3815A
frames a Furuno's NMEA, and the Z3815A's GPS and loop code that has no
counterpart here (`cut -f1-3,7` of the two TSVs, `applications/gps` and

base (`seed_names.py`'s `PORT_Z3816A_4001`; `make seed-names`).  Its
types start as the 58503A's `smartclock.h` and `check-layout.c`
(`rewritten/z3816a-4001/types/`), their layouts, addresses and comments
the 58503A's but where "Types" says otherwise.

address where the Z3816A's callers name them (the names file's header):
`output_state_program` and `pulse_program`, bare `rts`, at `0x46468` and
`0x4646a`, which `output_state_done` and `pulse_settings_done` call
(`0x464d2`, `0x464c0`); `duartb_echo_none` at `0x2e60e`, which

`installer_scpi_message_done` and `installer_scpi_input_closed` at
`installer::0x1074c2` and `installer::0x1074c6`, which
`installer_scpi_interpret` calls.  It corrects eight pairings

`0x4f720`, its last instructions, the 58503A's code; `bit_4_set` and

`status_screen`'s health table (`0x5246c`, `0x5247a`);
`installer_parameter_record_read` is `installer::0x108356`, which
`installer_system_init` calls (`installer::0x100c84`), where image_diff
pairs its defaults, 8 bytes before it, as for the Z3815A, which a hand
row places, `installer_sci_settings_defaults` (`installer::0x10834e`);
`gps_pps_monitor_reset` and `internal_pps_monitor_reset` are `0x33ab6`
and `0x33c36`, which `pps_monitors_reset` calls (`0x33fc2`, `0x33fc6`),
where image_diff pairs `0x24070`, which clears five bytes as the
58503A's first does, and `0x2407c` inside it: `0x24070` is
`timecode_init`, as the Z3805A's and the Z3815A's diffs pair it, which
`primary_start` calls (`0x22e5a`) and which a hand row names, clearing
`tcode_length`, `tcode_pending`, `es_length`, `es_ascii` and
`es_enabled`; and `scpi_input_source` is `0x39480`, which the SCPI handlers call
(`scpi_query_failed`'s, `0x38fc2`), where image_diff pairs `0x3943c`,
`port_object`'s last instructions, `moveq #0` and `rts`, by their bytes;
and `duart_early_init` is `0x2dd64`, which `primary_reset` calls
(`0x24aa2`), as the 58503A's calls its own (58503A `0x14958`), where
image_diff pairs the boot code's copy, `boot_duart_early_init`, the

the 58503A's gives 0 ("The link check").  Two words before
`psos_config_defaults`, `0x581b4` and `0x581b6`, which the NV

The Z3815A shares the Z3816A's layout ("Layout and chip selects") and

the second base"; `make seed-names` lists them under "Rows from the
second base".  The other seeded images carry less of the primary than
either (`python3 -I tools/firmware/image_diff.py` against each, as the
firmware Makefile's `image_diff_run` runs it).

A third diff, against the Z3805A 3543B
([`diff/z3816a-4001-vs-z3805a-3543b.tsv`](diff/z3816a-4001-vs-z3805a-3543b.tsv)),
is kept beside the two for `common/`, which came from the Z3805A's C:
`make check-common` compares each image sharing `common/` with the
Z3805A by its diff, and `make report-link` marks a missing function
`common/` would fit by it (`rewrite.md`, "Shared C").  It seeds nothing;
`make check-image-diff` checks it with the others.

Two cautions for anyone reading the seeded names or a Ghidra listing
made with them:

- A changed or lead row keeps the 58503A's name for code that may do
  something else here.
- Records still typed with the 58503A's `smartclock.h` are its layouts
  over the Z3816A's bytes: a field name in a listing says where the
  58503A keeps that field, not what the Z3816A's byte there is.

## Layout and chip selects

**Read.**  The boot code and the USA installer fill the first 128 KiB:
the installer's records at `0x8000` (`restart.md`, "The installer"),
unpacked to `0x100400` and entered at `0x1013a2` (boot vector 43,
`0x85a`; the jump at `0x8b6`), the primary's vectors at `0x20000`.  The
Z3815A's installer is entered at `0x1013a6`, its code 28 bytes on
(`restart.md`).

The chip selects are `README.md`'s table, which the Z3815A 4010's
primary writes value for value ([`chip-selects.md`](chip-selects.md),
"Z3816A 4001").

## The host port

**Read.**  The primary's I/O table, which `psos_config_init` (`0x57fc6`)
copies from `0x58670` to `0x102c90`, has the 58503A's three devices,
but its device 0 is the SCI, as the Z3817A's is
([`z3817a-3752.md`](z3817a-3752.md), "SCPI and the console: the SCI"),
not DUART channel B: its initialization, `sci_init` (`0x2ed56`),
creates `sciR` and `sciW` (`0x2ee16`), sets SCCR0 from the settings'
baud byte (`sci_baud_set`, `0x2e610`, called at `0x2ed92`) and SCCR1
from their parity byte, and `sci_putc_polled` and `sci_putc` write SCDR
(`0x2e6a2`) and SCCR1 (`README.md`).  Device 1 is the QSPI and device 2
the string device, whose write is the SCI's (`sci_write`, `0x2f10e`).
The SCPI session and the console's string device are on the SCI.
DUART channel B's registers are touched only by the DUART's interrupt
handler, `duart_stop`, its self-tests and the boot code's set-up
(`duarta_isr`, `duart_test_rx_b`, `duartb_loopback_check`,
`duart_early_init`).

The seeding named the SCI's driver by the 58503A's DUART channel B
routines it took the place of (`duart_init`, `drtb_putc`,
`duartb_putc_polled` and the rest).  Hand rows give them the Z3815A
4010's names (`sci_*`, `installer_sci_*`), each by image_diff's
pairing with the Z3815A's (`diff/z3816a-4001-vs-z3815a-4010.tsv`) or
its place among them, and place the driver's state where its code
addresses it: `sci_settings` at `0x10261a`, which `sci_settings_preset`
copies 6 bytes over (`0x406aa`); `sci_exchanges` at `0x1036b0`, which
`sci_lock` and `sci_unlock` take (`0x2ebde`); `sci_ring` at `0x1036e8`;
and `sci` at `0x1037ea`, the 0x1e bytes from there to the QSPI's state
(`0x103808`, `qspi_init`), where the seeding placed the 58503A's
`duartb`, 0x56 bytes, at `0x1037b2`; and the installer's,
`installer_sci_ring` at `0x10aee8`, `installer_sci` at `0x10afea` and
`installer_sci_settings` at `0x10ac94`.  The seeding's `duartb` and
`installer_duartb_receive_errors`, inside `installer_sci`, are dropped
(`seed_names.py`'s `DROPPED_Z3816A`).  In `modules.tsv` hand rows,
each in place of the seeded row of its address, move the driver, its
settings' done hook (`duartb_settings_done`, which controls device 0)
and their changed hooks (`duartb_parity_restrict` and
`duartb_eight_bits_restrict`, which keep `sci_settings`) from
`devices/duartb` to `devices/sci`.

**Read.**  The primary's `sci` is the Z3815A's `sci_driver` but for its
transmitting flags: this image's `sci_putc_polled` marks nothing
transmitting and schedules no end (`0x2e660`), so the character time,
the echo and the last terminator are 2 bytes lower (`sci_baud_set`'s
store at `0x2e610`, `sci_init`'s of the echo at `0x2efa0`).  The
installer's `installer_sci` is laid out as the Z3815A's primary's
`sci`, with them (`installer_sci_transmitting_set`, `installer::0x103038`;
`installer_sci_putc_polled`, `installer::0x10315e`), where the Z3815A's
installer keeps a byte of its own at `+0x0c`: `smartclock.h`'s
`sci_driver` and `installer_sci_driver`.

relative to the output, so an export written elsewhere and copied in
names them by paths `check-alone` does not read.  Ghidra refuses a
project path with an element that starts with a dot.

The image shares `common/` since the join ("Joining `common/`",
below).  Until then its export took `headers=` in place of the first
`rewritten=`, which had the export include common/'s headers and
bindings and none of its C; the join dropped each copy of what
`common/` defines.  Its export leaves out five files of `common/`
(`without=`), every function of which it lacks (`rewrite.md`, "Shared
C", "A file for some images"): `common/bytes_equal.c`,
`common/console_pr_1pps.c`, `common/duartb_settings.c`,
`common/installer/installer_duart_transmit.c` and `common/pwma.c`.

Z3816A's: `os.c`,

`bindings/clock_record.h` is the Z3815A's `clock_record_derive`, which
the Z3816A's code matches.  `results.tsv` is the shared table.

Hand rows (the names file's "row added by hand for the Z3816A 4001")
give the export what it needs to compile: the kernel's configuration
and I/O tables, `psos_config` and `io_table`, and the installer's,

`gps_response_cursor`, where `gps_response_reset` stores;
`pll`, the loop's state, which `pll_init` clears and `system_init`

`scpi_param_DOUGlas_STATe_request`, the descriptor

- `gps_state`'s `engine` (`+0x608`, `0x1014c4` its high byte), a word
  of what the task knows of its engine (`gps_engine_bit`), which the
  snapshot copies whole (`0x4ecec`).  `@@Cj`'s decoder sets it by the
  receiver ID's model line, the character after "MODEL #    "
  (`0x55d78` to `0x55e4e`): B, a VP (`VPCommands.pdf`, "Identification
  by model number"), sets bit 9 and runs `gps_engine_vp_set`
  (`0x54c24`); R, a UT Plus, bit 10 and `gps_engine_ut_plus_set`
  (`0x54bea`); any other, or a line not found, clears both and runs
  `gps_engine_unknown_set` (`0x54c54`).  The software, its version
  times 10 plus its revision (`0x55d16` to `0x55d44`), decides three
  bits by unsigned compares: a VP's of 10.0 or later sets bit 8, which
  lets `@@Aq`'s encoder send modes 2 and 3 (`0x55da0`, `0x54b02`), and
  of 8.4 the low byte's bit 7, the second `@@Ar`'s filter (`0x55de2`,
  `0x5608e`); a UT Plus's of 3.0 or later sets bit 14, `@@Am`'s filter,
  and the low byte's bit 6, `@@Gj`'s (`0x55e0c`, `0x55e24`, `0x5607e`,
  `0x560a2`), `@@Gj` having come with the UT Plus's software 3.0
  (febo.com/pipermail/time-nuts/2006-April/020639.html).  Bit 13, `@@Bn`'s and `@@En`'s filter (`0x5605a`), is a
