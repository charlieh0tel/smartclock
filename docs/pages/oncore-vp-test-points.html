Oncore VP Test Points

The receiver's MC68331CFC16 (132-pin PQFP) has no BDM header; a programming fixture's BDM set comes out on test points along the board edge. "To confirm" marks what came from a photo or one buzz-out.

Test points

Board DENU9621. Pin 1 is at the top center of the package, out of frame, so the lower edge runs 51 (left) to 83 (right). Traces were followed on a second board, DEVT9614, taken to share the layout.

BERR (pin 70) and DS (pin 85, PE5/DS) have no test point. The resistors below the lower edge are 10 kΩ ("103") pull-ups: on 55 (DSI) and 56 (BKPT) to one via; on 57 or 58 to a bus joining 61, 63 and 65 (VDDSYN, VDD); on 69 (HALT), 70 (BERR), 71–77 (IRQ7–IRQ1, tied together) and 78 (MODCLK).
